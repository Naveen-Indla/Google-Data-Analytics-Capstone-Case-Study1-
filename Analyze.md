Data Analysis Insights
1. Rider Distribution by Bike Type
  - Analysis was performed to understand which bike types are preferred by casual riders and members.
Insights
    - Members primarily use classic bikes and electric bikes.
    - Casual riders show slightly higher usage of electric bikes, possibly due to convenience.
Business Insight
    - Electric bikes may attract casual riders who prefer easier rides.

2. Monthly Ride Trends
  - Trips were aggregated by month and user type to identify seasonal patterns.
Insights
    - Ride activity increases significantly during summer months (June–August).
    - Casual riders show higher seasonal variation compared to members.
    - Members maintain relatively stable usage throughout the year.
Business Insight
    - Casual riders are more active during warmer months, suggesting seasonal tourism and leisure rides.

3. Ride Frequency by Day of the Week
  - Ride counts were analyzed by weekday to observe riding patterns.
Insights
    - Casual riders tend to ride more on weekends (Saturday and Sunday).
    - Members ride more frequently on weekdays.
Business Insight
    - Marketing strategies for casual riders could focus on weekend promotions.

4. Ride Activity by Hour of the Day
  - Ride counts were grouped by hour of the day.
Insights
    - Members show peak usage during commuting hours:
        - Morning: 7 AM – 9 AM
        - Evening: 4 PM – 6 PM

    -Casual riders show higher usage during afternoon and evening hours.
    -Casual riders have a more evenly distributed riding pattern throughout the day.
Business Insight
      -Members primarily use the service for commuting, while casual riders use it for recreational trips.

5. Average Ride Duration by Bike Type
  - Average ride length was analyzed for each vehicle type and user type.
Insights
    - Casual riders typically have longer ride durations than members.
    - Members usually take shorter rides, consistent with commuting behavior.
    -Electric bikes slightly reduce average ride duration due to faster travel speed.
Business Insight
    - Casual riders tend to use bikes for longer recreational rides.

6. Average Ride Duration by Month
  -Average ride duration was compared across months and user types.
Insights
    - Casual riders maintain longer rides during summer months.
    -Members’ ride duration remains relatively consistent across months.
Business Insight
    -Seasonal factors influence casual rider behavior more strongly than member behavior.

7. Average Ride Duration by Day
  - Ride duration was evaluated by day of the week.
Insights
    -Casual riders take longer rides on weekends.
    - Members maintain similar ride durations throughout the week.
Business Insight
    - Weekend rides are more leisure-oriented, particularly for casual riders.

8. Average Ride Duration by Hour
  -Ride duration was analyzed by hour of the day.
Insights
    - Casual riders take longer rides in midday and evening hours.
    - Members take shorter rides during morning and evening commuting periods.
Business Insight
    - Members primarily use bikes for transportation, while casual riders use them for exploration or leisure.

Key Overall Findings:
Members use bikes mainly for commuting
Casual riders use bikes mainly for recreation.
Casual riders ride longer and more often on weekends.
Members ride more frequently during weekday commuting hours
Seasonal patterns strongly affect casual rider behavior.
